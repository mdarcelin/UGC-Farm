UGC Agent Farm

Your control panel for UGC video runs: intake, character vault, solo agents, and queue.

Please read first: this dashboard is your control panel only. It holds your intake form, your Character Vault, your solo agent forms, and your Queue. It does not browse Pinterest, generate images, or make videos by itself, and it never posts anything. The real work happens with you in chat: press Run in Chat on a Queue card, copy the prompt, and your AI runs the farm from there and brings the finished video back to you for approval. Nothing is ever posted automatically. Posting always needs your separate approval.

How the farm works

  1. Set up your characters. Add each locked character in the Character Vault with a Slot 1 headshot and a Slot 2 body reference sheet. A character only counts as locked when both slots are filled.
  2. Start a run. Fill in the Video Intake Form for a full farm run, or use a Solo Agent Form when you only need one agent job.
  3. Find it in the Queue. Every run and solo job lands in the Queue, newest first. The status buttons there are labels you set by hand - they never run anything by themselves.
  4. Run it in chat. Press Run in Chat on the card, copy the prompt, and paste it into your chat with your AI. The farm runs with you there, step by step.
  5. Approve the video. The finished video comes back to you in chat for your approval. It is never posted, published, or shared without you saying so.

The 5 agents

A full run moves through all five, in this order. Each one also has its own solo form on the Solo Agents page.

1

Pinterest Scout

Searches Pinterest in guest view, no sign-in, for outfits and products for the chosen mode and topic - especially products with an Amazon link. Picks the outfit or product and copies its picture. Does not check price or availability at this step.

2

Image Agent

Creates the person and avatar images in chat: character references, the outfit photo of your locked character wearing the exact Pinterest outfit, and the outfit reference sheet. All ultra realistic, real skin and hair texture, all PNG, all 9:16.

3

Story Agent

Writes the hook, the shots, and the script. Labels every scene with the run's one Product Presentation choice. Writes scene action only for video prompts - never a description of the character.

4

Video Agent

Makes the video in chat with your AI video tool, using the Consistent Character workflow below. Returns the finished video in chat for your approval. Never posts it.

5

Caption Agent

Writes the TikTok caption, the on-screen hook, and hashtags. No price, discount, or sale claim unless a live price was actually verified.

Product Presentation: one choice, never both

Every run picks exactly one Product Presentation, right after the Mode on the intake form:

Wearing the product: every scene prompt ends "Wearing the product, not holding it."
Holding the product: every scene prompt ends "Holding the product, not wearing it."
  • Never show her wearing an outfit while holding that same outfit on a hanger.
  • Reject and regenerate any image that shows both at once.
  • If a video needs both, use separate scenes - holding first, wearing later - never the same shot.

The workflow, every run

  1. Pick the outfit on Pinterest - one with an Amazon link when there is a product. Copy its picture.
  2. Lock the outfit in chat, not in the video tool. Using your locked character's reference photos, put that character in that exact outfit in chat first. Check the face match and the outfit match.
  3. Make the outfit reference sheet in chat. From the approved outfit photo, one single 9:16 sheet with front, profile, and three-quarter shots of her in that outfit, all on the same photo. Save as PNG.
  4. Build the video with the Consistent Character lock. Reference One is her headshot (Slot 1). Reference Two is the chat-made outfit reference sheet. Never leave Reference Two empty. No character bible is needed - the reference photos are the lock. Do not paste descriptions of her into scene prompts.
  5. Create the scenes from that lock, in script order, 9:16.
  6. Export and return the video in chat for your approval. Never post, publish, or share it.

The scene prompt shape - use this, and nothing else about her

"Use the reference images and keep the character perfectly consistent. [Scene action only.] [Spoken line, if any.] Wearing the product, not holding it."

For a Holding run, the last sentence swaps to: "Holding the product, not wearing it."

Never describe her in a scene prompt - no face, hair, skin, body, or outfit description. Describing her overrides the references and creates a different girl.

Standing rules

  • Every image your AI creates is PNG, never webp. Your own uploaded Vault photos stay exactly as you saved them (JPG or PNG).
  • Nothing is posted automatically, ever. Posting always needs your separate approval.
  • No price, discount, or sale claim in any caption or copy unless a live price was actually verified.
  • Within one video, identity, clothing, location, and lighting stay frozen from the reference photos.
  • Characters are ultra realistic, with real skin texture and real hair texture.
  • Voice and copy everywhere: personable, human, plain words.
